# German Solar Market Overview (synthetic example data)

Germany remains Europe's largest installed solar PV market. Rooftop segment
growth is driven by residential electricity prices and simplified grid
connection rules for systems under 30 kWp.

Key facts:

- Feed-in tariffs for small rooftop systems have declined steadily since 2012.
- Commercial rooftop tenders are oversubscribed, compressing achieved prices.
- Grid connection queues are the single most cited delay in project delivery.

The utility-scale segment depends on auction outcomes and land availability,
which vary significantly by federal state.
