Synthetic competitor notes for the Solar Market Analysis workspace.

Helios Energie GmbH operates in the residential rooftop segment and depends on
a single inverter supplier. Its installer network covers Bavaria and
Baden-Wuerttemberg only.

Nordlicht Solar focuses on commercial rooftop and has an in-house EPC team.
Nordlicht Solar depends on imported modules and is exposed to tariff changes.

Weser PV is a utility-scale developer. Weser PV competes with Nordlicht Solar
for grid connection capacity in northern Germany.
